Care Team

We work as a family health team. Your family physician is a key part of that team, but not always the first person you need to see. Our staff may suggest the team member best suited to help you.

Physician Visits

Your family physician is here for your regular care, including new health concerns, ongoing conditions and prescription renewals. Book online at any time, or call the office at 506-645-8888. We also keep a limited number of same-day appointments for new, sudden problems. See how appointments work for details.

Counselling

We are currently introducing counselling services at our clinic, through our new social worker. This service is offered at no cost to you, with the goal of providing timely access to support when you need it.

You do not need a referral or recommendation from your family physician to access counselling. Our clinic staff may also suggest counselling as a first point of contact when appropriate.

Our social worker works in collaboration with your family physician as part of your overall care team, helping support your health and well-being.

To learn more or ask about accessing counselling, please call our office at 506-645-8888.

Please note: counselling is not a crisis service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911. For crisis support:

  • 988 Suicide Crisis Helpline: call or text 988, 24 hours a day.
  • NB Addiction and Mental Health Helpline: 1-866-355-5550, 24 hours a day.
  • Mobile Crisis Services (Saint John area): 1-888-811-3664.

For other services and the latest contact numbers, see Horizon Addiction and Mental Health Services.

Cervical Screening Clinics

Our nurses are trained to do routine cervical screening. You can have it done by one of our nurses or by your own family physician. Call the office and tell us who you would prefer to see.

New Brunswick now screens with an HPV test. The sample is collected the same way as a Pap test. For most people with normal results:

  • Start at 25 if you have a cervix and have ever been sexually active (any genital contact, with partners of any gender).
  • Every 5 years if your HPV test is negative.
  • Stop after 69 if your screening over the past 10 years was normal.
  • You still need screening if you are pregnant, past menopause, have had the HPV vaccine, or had a hysterectomy that left the cervix in place.
  • You can stop if your cervix was removed (total hysterectomy) for a reason other than cancer and you have never had abnormal cervical cells or HPV.

If a result is not normal, your physician will explain what happens next.

Higher risk: talk to your physician

  • A weakened immune system (organ transplant, HIV, or medicines that suppress the immune system): screening is every 3 years instead of 5.
  • Past high-grade abnormal cells or cervical cancer: follow the plan from your specialist.
  • Symptoms at any age, such as unusual bleeding, pain during sex, or unusual or ongoing discharge: this needs an assessment, not a screening test. Book with your family physician.

Please note: nursing visits are for routine screening only. If you have symptoms or concerns, please book with your family physician.

Diabetes Educator Clinics

To help guide diabetic patients in our practice with the management of their diabetes, we have the services of two Diabetes Educators, a specially trained dietitian and nurse, on Fridays at our clinic. Visits are by appointment only. To request one, call our admin team at 506-645-8888.

Vaccinations

Our nurses provide all vaccine care at the clinic. We stock all routine childhood and adult vaccines, apart from those given in school and the COVID-19 vaccine, which is available at pharmacies. If you would like to know which vaccines are recommended for you, ask to speak with one of our nurses. See Staying Healthy for the adult schedule.

Other Services

Our nurses can help you with other services without needing to see your family physician. Examples include ear syringing, routine injections (Vitamin B12, DepoProvera) and dressing changes. Contact our office and ask to book a nursing appointment for one of these reasons.

Our Partnership with Horizon Health Network

We have partnered with Horizon Health Network as a Collaborative Care Clinic. Through this partnership, counselling with a social worker is now available at our clinic. We hope to add care from other health professionals, such as physiotherapists, occupational therapists and respiratory therapists. These services aren’t available yet.
