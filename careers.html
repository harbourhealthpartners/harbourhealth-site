About Harbour Health Partners

Harbour Health Partners is a multi-physician primary care medical practice located in Millidgeville, in Saint John, New Brunswick.

Our mission: To be a medical home that patients can fully trust. This means that we are available, approachable, and provide safe, responsive, and science-based care.

Our values:

  • Community Needs Responsiveness
  • Secure Skilled Staff
  • Collaborative Team Based Approach
  • Inclusive Safe Environment
  • Education Focused
  • Innovation Seeking

The role

We are looking for a reliable, skilled, and proactive Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN) to join our nursing team. Our nursing professionals play an essential role in providing direct patient care, supporting our physicians, maintaining efficient patient flow, and contributing to the daily operations of our clinic.

This is a unique, hands-on position that combines independent nursing appointments with scheduled Clinical Assistant (CA) coverage. We are looking for someone who takes initiative, communicates effectively, anticipates the needs of others, and enjoys working collaboratively in a busy primary care environment.

The person we are looking for

The ideal candidate is a caring, professional, self-directed nursing professional who enjoys both direct patient care and the operational aspects of a busy medical practice.

You should have:

  • Strong clinical judgment, assessment skills, and attention to detail.
  • Excellent organizational and time-management skills.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills in English.
  • The ability to work independently, anticipate needs, and take initiative without continuous direction.
  • The flexibility to transition between assigned nursing and CA responsibilities and respond quickly to changing clinical priorities.
  • A compassionate, respectful, and professional approach to patient care.
  • The ability to collaborate effectively with physicians, nursing colleagues, administrative staff, and management.
  • Confidence using electronic medical records and clinical equipment.
  • Strong problem-solving abilities and the ability to recognize when escalation or additional support is required.
  • Reliability, including regular attendance and adherence to scheduled hours.
  • A willingness to participate in shared responsibilities, ongoing learning, and quality improvement initiatives.

  • Demonstrated ability to manage competing priorities, work independently, and collaborate within a healthcare team.

Shared clinical and operational responsibilities

Full job description Duties, expectations and physical requirements

Integrated Nursing & Clinical Support (LPN) · Harbour Health Nursing Team

Position overview

The CA/LPN plays an essential role in supporting patient care, efficient patient flow, and daily clinical operations at Harbour Health Partners.

This position combines the responsibilities of a Clinical Assistant and Licensed Practical Nurse, with duties performed according to established schedules and assigned clinical responsibilities. The employee will have assigned schedules for independent nursing appointments and separate schedules for CA coverage. Responsibilities will be coordinated with the nursing team and management to ensure appropriate clinical coverage, continuity of care, and efficient clinic operations.

The role requires strong clinical skills, proactive communication, organization, adaptability, and the ability to transition effectively between nursing and CA responsibilities as scheduled.

Physical requirements

The CA/LPN position involves regular physical activity throughout the workday. The employee must be able to:

  • Stand, walk, and sit for varying periods throughout the workday.
  • Frequently move between examination rooms, nursing areas, and other clinical spaces.
  • Bend, reach, lift, carry, and move clinical supplies and equipment as required.
  • Assist patients with mobility, dressing, positioning, and movement, following safe patient-handling practices.
  • Perform clinical procedures requiring manual dexterity, coordination, and precision, including vaccinations, blood collection, wound care, and cervical screening.
  • Maintain appropriate positioning and physical control when performing clinical procedures and assisting patients.
  • Perform cleaning, examination room preparation, equipment maintenance, and other physical tasks associated with daily clinical operations.

Professional expectations and accountability

  • Demonstrate reliability, punctuality, professionalism, and strong organizational and clinical skills.
  • Work independently, recognize what needs to be done, and take initiative without continuous direction.
  • Communicate clearly, respectfully, and proactively with patients, physicians, nursing colleagues, MOAs, and management.
  • Work collaboratively with the nursing team, sharing clinical responsibilities and coordinating patient care and coverage according to assigned schedules.
  • Manage competing priorities, adapt quickly to changing clinical demands, and transition effectively between assigned nursing and CA responsibilities.
  • Maintain accuracy, attention to detail, and timely completion of clinical documentation, patient care activities, and assigned responsibilities.
  • Demonstrate sound clinical judgment, recognize the limits of their professional scope, training, and competencies, and escalate concerns appropriately.
  • Maintain current LPN registration and comply with applicable professional standards, ethical obligations, and continuing competency requirements.
  • Maintain required certifications and continuing education, proactively communicating certification status, renewals, completed training, and professional development activities to management.
  • Follow established clinic policies, clinical procedures, infection prevention and control standards, and occupational health and safety requirements.
  • Maintain strict patient confidentiality and comply with applicable privacy and Public Health requirements, including immunization recommendations.
  • Be receptive to feedback, ongoing learning, professional development, and changes in clinic processes.
  • Participate in team meetings, training, and quality improvement initiatives.
  • Contribute to a collaborative, respectful, and supportive team environment.

Essential job functions and responsibilities

These include, but are not limited to:

Nursing and independent patient care (independent schedule)

  • Manage scheduled nursing appointments independently, providing patient assessments, treatments, and clinical services within the LPN’s authorized scope of practice, training, and competencies.
  • Administer adult and infant immunizations in accordance with established protocols and applicable Public Health requirements.
  • Perform cervical screening (Pap tests), as authorized and appropriately trained.
  • Provide wound care, dressing changes, and related patient education.
  • Perform ear irrigation (ear syringing) when clinically appropriate.
  • Conduct cognitive screening assessments, including MoCA and MMSE, in accordance with training and applicable requirements.
  • Perform blood collection and associated specimen handling.
  • Participate in infant developmental assessments and well-baby care in collaboration with physicians and the nursing team.
  • Provide patient education, clinical guidance, and follow-up within the LPN’s scope of practice.
  • Identify and communicate clinical concerns, abnormal findings, and required follow-up to the appropriate physician or nursing team member.
  • Coordinate nursing responsibilities and patient care with the nursing team to support continuity of care and appropriate clinical coverage.

Patient management and Clinical Assistant support (CA coverage)

  • Room and escort patients in accordance with the clinic’s established Patient Rooming Policy.
  • Assist patients with physical needs, including dressing, positioning, getting on and off examination tables, and travelling to and from their vehicles when required.
  • Prepare examination rooms, clinical equipment, supplies, and testing kits in advance of scheduled appointments and procedures.
  • Obtain and document patient measurements, including blood pressure readings, weight, and other measurements as directed.
  • Perform on-site testing, including urine pregnancy tests and urinalysis (urine dips), in accordance with established clinic procedures.
  • Assist physicians during examinations and procedures, including acting as a chaperone when required.
  • Complete assigned specimen collection, testing, and associated requisitions.
  • Ensure specimens are correctly labelled, packaged, and promptly handed over to the MOA team, with courier pickup arranged in a timely manner.
  • Maintain patient dignity, privacy, comfort, and safety during all interactions.

Clinical scheduling, patient flow and team coordination

  • Proactively review upcoming physician and nursing schedules to anticipate clinical needs, room requirements, and necessary preparations.
  • Plan and coordinate examination room assignments during assigned CA coverage, communicating arrangements and anticipated needs through established clinic communication channels.
  • Maintain clear, proactive communication with physicians and nursing staff regarding scheduled and unscheduled clinical needs throughout the day.
  • Respond promptly to on-demand clinical requests, demonstrating flexibility and the ability to adjust priorities as clinical needs change.
  • Coordinate with the nursing team and management regarding assigned nursing appointments, CA coverage, and shared clinical responsibilities.
  • Monitor patient flow and room availability, adjusting plans to accommodate changing schedules, unexpected procedures, and provider requests.
  • Communicate delays, competing priorities, patient concerns, and operational issues promptly to the appropriate team members.

Electronic Medical Records and clinical documentation

  • Accurately and promptly document nursing assessments, treatments, immunizations, patient measurements, test results, and other clinical information in the Electronic Medical Record (EMR).
  • Create, assign, monitor, and complete EMR tasks in accordance with established clinic workflows.
  • Maintain accurate and complete documentation of independent nursing appointments and associated follow-up.
  • Ensure required clinical forms, requisitions, and documentation are completed appropriately.
  • Communicate outstanding tasks, abnormal findings, discrepancies, or incomplete documentation to the appropriate provider.
  • Maintain patient confidentiality and follow established privacy and documentation policies.

Space and supply management

Coordination and accountability

  • Nursing team members are expected to self-coordinate and collaboratively distribute responsibilities for clinical space maintenance, inventory management, stocking, and supply ordering.
  • Ensure responsibilities are clearly assigned within the team, including a designated lead for inventory and ordering, to maintain accountability and prevent duplication or missed tasks.
  • Communicate proactively with one another regarding supply needs, outstanding tasks, and competing priorities to ensure responsibilities are completed in a timely manner.
  • Escalate unresolved concerns, supply shortages, or coordination challenges to management as required.

Clinical spaces, cleaning and maintenance

  • Maintain clean, organized, functional, and appropriately stocked examination rooms, nursing areas, and clinical workspaces.
  • Perform daily, weekly, and monthly cleaning and disinfection duties in accordance with clinic protocols and infection prevention and control standards.
  • Monitor clinic floors, particularly during winter months, using provided mops to address tracked-in snow, water, and debris. Communicate with MOAs when additional assistance is required.
  • Identify and report equipment, facility, maintenance, or supply concerns to management.
  • Perform delegated monthly equipment maintenance according to established procedures.

Inventory and supply ordering

  • Monitor clinical inventory levels and ensure supplies are available to support daily operations.
  • Coordinate weekly supply ordering through approved vendors.
  • Coordinate SPD supply orders through Horizon Health Network and linen orders through Service New Brunswick.
  • Receive, organize, and appropriately store incoming supplies.
  • Monitor product expiry dates, supply shortages, and replacement requirements.

Administrative and general housekeeping

  • Complete timesheets bi-weekly.
  • Support general clinic opening and closing responsibilities as assigned.
  • Maintain clean, organized, and professional shared workspaces and staff areas.
  • Assist with general clinic organization, supplies, and operational needs.
  • Participate in team meetings, training, and quality improvement initiatives.
