Patient Resources

Trusted health information from organizations we rely on. These links open in a new tab and are maintained by others, not by our clinic.

  • 211 New Brunswick

    Call 2-1-1 for help finding community and social services, such as food, housing, financial help and mental health support. Free, confidential, and available 24 hours a day in over 150 languages.

  • Tele-Care 811

    Call 811 any time, day or night, to speak with a nurse about a health concern.

  • Virtual Care NB

    Virtual appointments for common, non-urgent problems, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., seven days a week.

  • Mental health services in New Brunswick

    In a crisis, call or text 988, or call the NB Addiction and Mental Health Helpline at 1-866-355-5550 (24 hours a day).

  • New Brunswick cancer screening programs

    Breast, cervical, colon and lung screening. Information line: 1-844-777-3443.

  • New Brunswick Immunization Schedule

    Routine vaccines for children and adults.

  • Choosing Wisely New Brunswick

    Reducing unnecessary tests & treatments in health care.

  • College of Family Physicians of Canada

    Patient education resources.

  • Government of Canada: Travel health and safety

    Vaccines, medications and health advice for travellers, by destination.

  • Living Well with COPD

    Helps physicians and healthcare professionals partner with patients to promote self-management of their disease.

  • Diabetes Canada

    You can live an active, independent and vital life by carefully managing your diabetes.

  • Sex & U

    An initiative of the Society of Obstetricians and Gynaecologists of Canada, Canada’s leading authority on sexual and reproductive health.
